AFTERBURN

Prototype 23 · Frontier Conquest

Sound effects generated with ElevenLabs.

Operation

One side attacks through the sectors of a field, one after another, and the other holds them. You command your troops, and you are also in the fight. Press Tab during the fight for the battle map.

Private match

Two to six players, in two sides. One of you hosts and gets a code; the others type the code and join. Each of you brings your own faction, pilot, Titan and battlegroup. The host's side attacks or defends as chosen here, and everyone plays on the host's field. If someone drops out, opening this page again puts them back in.

The field

Every field is made from a kind and a number, so the same pair always makes the same field. Each one is walked by a test before you see it, and made again if troops could not reach a point. Building a field loads this page again.

Controls

TabThe battle map: deploy, pick squads, give orders
W A S DMove
ShiftSprint. In a Titan, Shift is a dash.
SpaceJump. Press it again in the air for a second jump.
Left CtrlSlide while you are sprinting, crouch while you are slow (C works too)
MouseLook, and hold the left button to fire
1, 2, 3Your primary, the rail-charge rifle, your sidearm
Right mouseAim down the sights. In a Titan it fires the rocket pod
QYour tactical kit: the grapple hook or the stim. Q again, or a jump, lets go of the grapple
RReload
XYour ordnance. Hold to cook a frag and let go to throw it; an arc grenade is thrown at once
GTap, or click the middle mouse button: your squads within 35 metres go where you are looking. Hold for a wheel and flick the mouse: up for nearby, left for core infantry, right for specialists, down for every squad not locked to a point, and down twice to take the locked ones too
Z, BCall Fireteam Alpha, call Fireteam Beta. You put cards in a fireteam in your battlegroup
FKnife on foot
VCall your Titan down where you are looking
EClimb into your mech when you are beside it
In a mechLeft button fires the autocannon. Q (or the right button) is the missile salvo, E the Vortex Wall, F the sensor pulse, X the core once it is charged. V is the melee. Shift or Space dashes. Hold B to get out
On the mapLeft click a squad's card to pick it, or drag a box round several. Left click the ground or a point to send them there. Hold Shift and click to add stops on the way, then let go of Shift and click where they should end up. Right drag moves the map, middle drag turns it.
